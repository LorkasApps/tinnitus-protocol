## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Tinnitus Protocol is a private journal for everyone living with tinnitus. Log how loud and how distressing your tinnitus feels throughout the day, record last night's sleep, and tag entries with potential triggers. Over time the app turns those small observations into clear patterns — so you can spot what actually moves the needle instead of guessing.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no ads, no trackers. The entire database is a local SQLite file that you can export or wipe whenever you want.

QUICK DAILY LOGGING
• Tinnitus entries: rate loudness (1–10) and distress (1–10), add free-text notes, log as often as you like throughout the day.
• Sleep log: one entry per day, quality (1–10) plus notes — perfect for tracking the night before.
• Tag entries with built-in triggers (stress, loud sound, caffeine, alcohol, lack of sleep, weather, screen time, exercise, medication, headache) or create your own custom tags.

SEE YOUR TRENDS
• Trend charts over the last 7, 30, 90 days or your full history.
• Switch between per-entry view and daily averages.
• Dedicated sleep chart with one point per day.
• Time-of-day heatmap shows when tinnitus tends to flare — morning, midday, afternoon, evening or night.
• Key figures table with min, mean, median and max for every metric.

FIND WHAT REALLY AFFECTS YOU
• Trigger impact: see how each tag changes average loudness and distress compared to entries without it, including effect size and sample count.
• Sleep correlation: Pearson r between sleep quality and tinnitus on paired days — quantify the link instead of guessing.
• Sleep-bucket comparison: tinnitus levels grouped by poor, medium and good nights.
• Trigger × time-of-day matrix highlights the worst combinations.
• Tag co-occurrence reveals which triggers tend to cluster together.

OWN YOUR DATA
• Export everything as CSV (two files, ready for Excel or Numbers) or JSON (single file, machine-readable) via the system share sheet.
• Import a previous JSON export to restore your history or move between devices.
• Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it yourself.

DESIGNED TO FIT YOUR DAY
• Material 3 interface, follows your system dark or light theme automatically.
• Available in English and German, picked from your system language.
• Lightweight and fast, no background services draining your battery.

WHO IT'S FOR
• Anyone with chronic or intermittent tinnitus who wants more than a vague feeling about "good" and "bad" days.
• People preparing for an ENT, audiologist or therapist appointment and looking for solid, structured notes.
• Sleep-sensitive tinnitus sufferers who want to confirm (or rule out) the sleep–tinnitus link with their own data.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
• No internet permission required for normal use.
• You decide if and when to share an export.

Tinnitus Protocol is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. It is a tracking tool to help you spot patterns over time.
